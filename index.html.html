Who We Are

A national NGO, founded in 2008

Concern and Care for the Needy in Rwanda (CCN-RWANDA) is committed to addressing social and economic challenges affecting vulnerable populations. Legally registered in 2017 (No. 175/RGB/NGO/LP/12/2017) and based in Kigali, we are active in Rwanda, DRC and Burundi, led by Country Coordinator Ephrem NTEZIRYAYO.

Our Vision

A Just, Inclusive and Prosperous Society

A society where all people enjoy improved social welfare, economic prosperity, equal opportunities, dignity, justice, and a healthy environment, free from poverty, discrimination, and social injustice.

Our Mission

Empowering Communities for Sustainable Development

To improve social welfare and socio-economic development by promoting access to education, healthcare, justice, human rights, environmental protection, conflict resolution, and economic opportunities, while empowering communities to build resilient, peaceful, and sustainable livelihoods.
